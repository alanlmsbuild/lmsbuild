## What the guidelines require (page 2 and 3, quoted)

Page 2, "Licence condition": "You must acknowledge National Rail as the source
of Information in accordance with this document, including Developer brand
Guidelines".

Page 3, "Attribution": "The user of NRE feeds (You) must acknowledge NRE as the
source of information, where possible, by providing a link to the NRE website
for more information and by displaying the \"Powered by National Rail
Enquiries\" logo in accordance with the Brand Guidelines and the following
conditions:

- Where an NRE feed is displayed as the predominant or standalone information
  in Your product or service, NRE attribution should be shown directly
  alongside, above or underneath the Information derived from the feed;
- Where an NRE feed is combined with other data feeds and/or is not the primary
  information being displayed, NRE branding may be shown on an attribution
  page;
- Where NRE feeds are displayed on a dot matrix screen, the words \"Powered by
  National Rail Enquiries\" should be used as static or scrolling text."

"If you are in any doubt about your proposed attribution, you can contact us
for clarity by e-mailing: NationalRailDatafeeds@raildeliverygroup.com

In some circumstances it may not be possible to display the NRE branding in
accordance with these recommendations or, in Your view, it would have a
detrimental impact on the end-user experience of the applicable product. In
such instances You should contact us to discuss alternatives and/or to be
granted an exemption. We will always endeavour to take a reasonable approach
when considering alternatives."

Pages 4 to 8 are about the logo (wordmark, colours blue, black or white, no
warping, 100% opacity, the "Powered by" version, the colour palette).

## What Rarebit does

The only wording the guidelines give for text is `Powered by National Rail
Enquiries` (for dot matrix screens, "as static or scrolling text"). Rarebit
credits in words only, no logo, so it uses those words with a link to the NRE
website, and asks NRE (the e-mail above) to confirm that words alone are
acceptable, or to grant the exemption the guidelines describe, before train
fares are shown to the public. Until NRE answers, nothing is published.
