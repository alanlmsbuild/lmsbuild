# NEET and participation age 16 to 17 by local authority: licence notes (decision 58)

Checked 30 October 2026. Loaded earlier (13 October 2026, load:data step 23); the licence is
recorded here with the silver and gold built on it.

- **Licence.** Open Government Licence v3.0. The release page
  (https://explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk/find-statistics/participation-in-education-training-and-neet-age-16-to-17-by-local-authority/2025-26)
  says "All content is available under the Open Government Licence v3.0, except where otherwise
  stated", and the release's data guidance says "This data is released under the terms of the
  Open Government License and is intended to meet at least three stars for Open Data".
  Publisher: Department for Education.
- **Attribution.** "Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government
  Licence v3.0. Department for Education." In every gold row (`attribution`), with the page
  (`source_url`) and the release's published date (`published_on`, 2 July 2026).
- **What it is.** Management information from local authorities' tracking of 16 and 17 year
  olds: participation in education and training (March count), and NEET and activity not
  known (an average of the December, January and February returns). Local authority,
  regional and England rows, by age, sex, ethnic group and SEND, 2019 to 2026. The releases
  2021/22 to 2025/26 were all checked: each holds the series from 2019, so the latest release
  has every year available on Explore education statistics. Earlier years (before 2019)
  are in the older GOV.UK publication "NEET and participation: local authority figures",
  which is not loaded.
- **Cautions the publisher gives, carried in gold (`caution`).** The figures are management
  information and local authorities differ in how well they track; NEET includes only those
  whose activity is known, so a high not-known share can change the rate; counts are rounded
  to the nearest 10 for national and regional figures; groups of 5 or fewer are suppressed (c);
  Peterborough's 2026 NEET and not-known data are suppressed (incomplete returns).
- **Personal data.** None: aggregated estimates, suppressed for small groups.
